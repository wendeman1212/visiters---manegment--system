17| * Visitor Transfer for Company Admin / CEO.
18| *
19| * Both roles are company-level users and may transfer a visitor to an eligible
20| * active Host — or to a company Admin/CEO — within any company they are
21| * authorized for. The modal follows a Company -> Branch -> Department cascade
22| * for Hosts. Company Admins/CEOs are company-level recipients and are offered
23| * as soon as a company is selected (never scoped by branch/department).
24| * Department lists the branch-scoped departments plus the company-level
25| * departments (branch_id NULL) so Hosts assigned to company-level departments
26| * stay reachable. Only eligible Hosts (role 'host', status 'active', assigned
27| * to the selected department and either company-level or stationed at the
28| * selected branch) are offered as Host recipients.
29| *
30| * Everything is validated against the live database relationships; no users or
31| * companies are hardcoded. The transfer is an Admin/CEO company-level action so
32| * it works independently of the Host-side transfer and the escalation flow.
33| */
